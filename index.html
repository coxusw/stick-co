What Stick Co Does

Cleanup jobs big and small.

Tyler handles the age-appropriate cleanup work. Dad handles power equipment, chainsaw work, and anything that needs an adult.

🌿

Stick & Limb Cleanup

Picking up fallen sticks, branches, and small limbs and getting the yard looking clean again.

🪵

Log Cleanup

Gathering, moving, stacking, and cleaning up logs and larger wood with adult help when needed.

🌲

Tree Debris Cleanup

Cleaning up debris around trees and after trimming. Any cutting requiring power equipment is handled by Dad.

🪨

Rock Spreading

Helping spread landscape rock and freshen up beds, borders, and other areas around the property.

🌱

Weed Cleanup

Pulling weeds and helping clean up overgrown landscape beds and other manageable areas.

🧹

Property Cleanup

Raking, hauling, gathering debris, and giving outdoor areas a general cleanup and refresh.

Meet the Boss

Tyler
Founder • Cleanup Crew Chief

Tyler is an outdoors kid who would rather be doing something than sitting around. He loves riding dirt bikes and four-wheelers, working outside, and especially cleaning up properties full of trees, logs, sticks, and brush.

He’s even gotten some carefully supervised seat time in a skid steer. Stick Co gives him a chance to turn that work ethic into his own little business while learning responsibility, customer service, and the value of earning money.
